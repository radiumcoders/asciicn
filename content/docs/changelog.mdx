## Backgrounds

<p className="changelog-date">2026-10-26</p>

Seeded GPU shaders, drawn as ASCII in their own colours.

- **[ASCII Shader](/docs/backgrounds/ascii-shader).** Any seed, any field, as glyphs.
- **[Lattice](/docs/backgrounds/lattice).** Violet silk folding over itself.
- **[Mirage](/docs/backgrounds/mirage).** Blue ribbons bending across the page.
- **[Monsoon](/docs/backgrounds/monsoon).** Teal arcs sweeping through a dark field.
- **[Velvet](/docs/backgrounds/velvet).** Red smoke drifting slowly.
